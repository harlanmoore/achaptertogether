Learn to meditate — at your own pace

Plain-language, research-grounded guides written for beginners, older adults, and people dealing with anxiety, grief, or sleep problems. No experience needed for any of them.

These guides grew out of the meditation coaching work Harlan does with clients — the questions people actually ask, the concerns that come up, and the things that genuinely help. Each one is honest about what the evidence does and doesn't support, and points toward a simple practice you can try today.
