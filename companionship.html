What this is

A Chapter Together started as a reading companion service, and reading is still at the heart of it. But what people kept telling me they valued most wasn't really the book — it was having someone show up, consistently, just to talk.

So this is that, openly: regular video visits with a real person who's glad to see you. We can read together if you'd like. We can talk about your week, your family, your garden, or whatever's on your mind. There's no fixed agenda — just company.

A note from Harlan

Since I was young, most of my friends have been older than me. I love listening to histories, stories, and the lessons that come from the older generation. I've learned from my older friends that finding someone who will really listen — someone who actually cares what they have to say — is hard to come by.

I appreciate you, and I'd love to chat. Reach out, and let's connect.

Questions

Do we have to read together?

Not at all. Reading is something we can do if you enjoy it, but plenty of visits are just conversation.

How often do people book?

However often feels right — weekly is common, but it's entirely up to you.

Is this just for people who live alone?

No — anyone who'd enjoy the company is welcome, whatever their situation.

What if my mom or dad isn't comfortable with video calls?

That's exactly the kind of thing the free first visit is for — we go at their pace, and I'll help however I can to make it easy.
