What we do

A Chapter Together is an online companionship service that pairs older adults with warm, screened companions for weekly one-on-one video visits.

Sessions combine conversation, storytelling, and shared reading to reduce loneliness and create meaningful social connection. Every companion is background-checked, interviewed, and trained. Families get a short check-in note after each visit so they know how their parent is doing.

1 in 3

Older adults feel lonely

According to the 2024 National Poll on Healthy Aging, roughly one in three adults aged 50–80 report feeling isolated. Loneliness is now recognized as a serious public health concern.

29%

Higher risk of heart disease

Studies from the American Heart Association link chronic loneliness in older adults to significantly higher rates of heart disease, stroke, and dementia.

15+

Minutes that change a day

Regular, meaningful contact — even briefly — measurably improves mood, cognition, and sleep. It's often the highlight of the week for our members.

About me

Hi, I'm Harlan.

I started A Chapter Together after losing my brother and my father on the same day. In the months that followed, I saw how much a steady, caring presence can mean — and how hard it is to find one when the people around you have moved on or don't know what to say.

I've spent fifteen years practicing Vipassana meditation, sitting with teachers in India and keeping up a daily practice through years of work and travel in remote places. That experience shaped everything about how we approach companionship here: patience, real listening, and an understanding that loneliness responds to two things — genuine connection with another person, and a steadier relationship with your own mind.

Every companion we bring on is someone I would trust with my own mother. That's the standard. If it doesn't feel right for your family in the first session, you don't owe us a thing.

— Harlan Moore, founder

Questions families ask

Everything you're probably wondering.

Is the first session really free?

Yes. The first session is free with no card required. If it's not a good fit, there's nothing to cancel and nothing to pay.

My parent isn't very tech-savvy. Will this work?

Most families use Zoom or FaceTime, and we send a simple one-tap link before each session. If needed, we'll do a short setup call so everything works before the first visit. Many members quickly grow to love the routine.

Are companions background-checked?

Yes. Every companion completes a background check, an in-person interview, and training on working warmly with older adults before they meet a single member. We match for personality, interests, and pace of conversation.

What if my parent has memory issues?

We work with many families whose parents have early-stage memory challenges. Our companions are trained in gentle, reassuring conversation and never rely on a member's ability to remember prior sessions. If more clinical care is needed, we'll say so honestly.

Can you send updates so I know how they're doing?

Yes. After each session you'll receive a short, warm note from the companion. It's not a clinical report — it's more like the kind of update a friend might send. Enough to know how your parent was, and often enough to give you something real to bring up next time you call.

Where in the world do you work?

We currently serve families in the United States, Canada, the United Kingdom, and Australia. If you're elsewhere, get in touch — we're expanding.
