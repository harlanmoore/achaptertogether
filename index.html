Hi, I'm Harlan Moore

"I used to read to my daughters every night before bed — and the best part was never the reading itself. It was the conversations afterwards. The questions, the laughter, the little debates. I started A Chapter Together to bring that same warmth and connection to seniors who deserve someone to share their thoughts with."

I've had what most people would call an unusual life. I built and sold several multimillion dollar companies — electronics, real estate. Made good money. And then one day I looked around and thought — is this it? So I left. I spent years as a gold buyer in Africa and the South Pacific. I've sat in villages most people will never find on a map, negotiated deals in languages I was still learning, and come home with stories I'm still processing. I've travelled to India, studied spirituality, and counted yogis among my closest friends. I now live in the jungles of Indonesia. I tell you all of this not to impress you — but because I think it matters who you invite into your week. Over the years I've lost older friends who were very dear to me. In their final months, I would go and read to them. Just sit with them, open a book, and read. I know — in a way that's hard to put into words — how much that meant. To them, and to me. Now I'd like to share that with you.

A virtual reading companion for seniors who love books

Senior loneliness is one of the most serious health challenges facing older adults in America today. One in four seniors lives alone, and 43% report feeling lonely — a level of isolation that affects memory, mood, and physical health. A Chapter Together was created to offer something simple and profoundly human: a real person, a good book, and a genuine conversation.

Unlike audiobooks or AI companions, our virtual reading sessions are live and personal. Every session is one-on-one with Harlan Moore — a real human being who shows up every week, remembers where you left off, and genuinely looks forward to hearing your thoughts. Sessions take place over Zoom or FaceTime, with no complicated setup required.

Research shows that seniors who participate in structured social reading activities report 34% lower rates of loneliness and 21% higher scores on cognitive assessments compared to those who don't. Reading together — and discussing what you've read — is one of the most effective and enjoyable ways to stay mentally sharp and emotionally connected.

Whether you're looking for a reading companion for an elderly parent, or you're a senior yourself who misses having someone to share a good book with — A Chapter Together offers a warm, personal, and affordable solution. Your first session is completely free.
